// about

Finding the voices hidden on the shelf!

Great books are published every day, but not every story gets the chance to find its audience. Somewhere on the shelf is an author you've never heard of, a story you didn't know you needed, and maybe your next favorite book.

Voices from the Shelf exists to find those voices and bring them into the conversation. We connect readers with indie authors through live conversations, buddy reads, honest recommendations, and a community built around discovering what might otherwise stay hidden.

  • Voices worth hearing Meet indie authors and discover the stories behind their stories.
  • Stories worth finding Books you might never see on the usual bestseller lists or endless feeds.
  • Conversations worth having Live interviews, buddy reads, book clubs, and discussions that go beyond "buy my book."
  • A shelf for everyone Different genres, different voices, and authors at every stage of the journey.
// questions

[FAQ]

Is submitting my book free?

Yes. Sending your book over for consideration costs nothing — no submission fee, no catch.

Does my book have to fit a specific genre?

No. Voices from the Shelf covers every genre, and authors at any stage — debut or tenth book.

What does the interview actually look like?

A live, unscripted conversation on TikTok Live — about the book, the process, and the story behind it. No pitch deck, no script.

How long until I hear back?

Around a week on average. Interview slots are limited each week, so the sooner you submit, the sooner you're on the calendar.
