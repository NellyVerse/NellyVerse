Privacy Policy

Last updated August 14, 2026

The short version

Voices from the Shelf collects only what you give us directly — mainly your email address and whatever you tell us in a book submission or newsletter signup. We don't sell your data, and we don't run tracking or advertising scripts on this site.

What we collect

We collect information you choose to give us:

  • Your email address, if you sign up for the newsletter.
  • Your name, email, and details about your book, if you submit it for consideration.
  • Anything you send us directly by email at collabs@voicesfromtheshelf.com.

We don't use cookies, analytics, or ad-tracking scripts on this site. Loading the page does pull fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as part of that request — that's standard for most websites and outside our control.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your submission, schedule interviews, send the newsletter you signed up for, and generally run Voices from the Shelf. We don't use your information for anything beyond that.

Third-party services

Newsletter signups and book submissions are handled through JotForm, a third-party form provider. When you submit a form on this site, your information is processed by JotForm on our behalf, subject to their own privacy policy. We don't share your information with any other outside company, and we don't sell it.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send. You can also email us at collabs@voicesfromtheshelf.com to ask what information we have about you or to request that it be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and change the "last updated" date above. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the update.

Questions

Reach out any time at collabs@voicesfromtheshelf.com.
